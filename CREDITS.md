# Credits

SerrebiTorrent exists because torrent clients are mouse-first and screen-reader-hostile. This one is keyboard-first and screen-reader-friendly: manage torrents locally with built-in libtorrent, or drive a remote qBittorrent, Transmission, or rTorrent from the same window.

Third-party projects it uses:

- libtorrent (built in locally).
- qBittorrent, Transmission, and rTorrent (remote clients; SCGI/XML-RPC).
- OpenSSL (ships in the ZIP).

Those clients belong to their own projects. SerrebiTorrent is a remote and local front end for them.

Questions and bug reports are welcome in the [SerrebiProjects Telegram group](https://t.me/SerrebiProjects). More serrebi projects: [projects.serrebiradio.com](https://projects.serrebiradio.com).
